Developer Support Policy

Effective Date: 27 July 2026

This Support Policy outlines the assistance available to Okoro users across supported app platforms and explains how to contact us.

Our Commitment

We strive to provide a high-quality experience by maintaining compatibility with supported operating systems and devices, addressing bugs and performance issues through app updates, responding to support requests in a timely and professional manner, and considering user feedback when improving the App.

Support Services

We can assist with installing and setting up the App, account and subscription enquiries where applicable, restoring purchases, questions about App features and functionality, reporting bugs or unexpected behaviour, and suggestions for improvements and new features.

Response Times

Our goal is to respond to general enquiries, technical issues, and critical functionality issues as soon as reasonably possible, usually within 2-5 business days. Response times may vary during public holidays or periods of high demand.

Before Contacting Support

To help us resolve your issue, please include your device model, operating-system version, App name and version, a clear description of the issue, steps that reproduce the problem, and screenshots or screen recordings if applicable.

Please do not send passwords, payment details, or other sensitive personal information.

Purchases and Subscriptions

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by the platform through which you obtained the App, such as the Apple App Store. For billing issues, refunds, or payment enquiries, contact the applicable platform provider directly. Developers cannot access your payment card information and may be unable to issue refunds on behalf of a platform.

We can assist with restoring purchases or troubleshooting access to paid features within the App.

Feature Requests

We welcome ideas that may improve Okoro. Although we cannot guarantee that every suggestion will be implemented, feedback may be considered during future development.

Software Updates

App updates may include bug fixes, performance improvements, new features, compatibility updates, and improvements to astronomical and environmental information. We recommend keeping automatic updates enabled to receive the latest improvements and compatibility fixes.

Service Availability

Some App features may rely on platform services or external providers, including weather, mapping, astronomical, tide, and environmental data services. Temporary interruptions may occur because of maintenance or circumstances beyond our control.
