Māori Data Sovereignty Policy

How Ōkoro protects the authority, dignity, context, and sovereignty of personal information and Māori data on Android.

Updated 23 November 2026

Our commitment: Personal and cultural information remains under the user’s control. The Developer does not operate a remote repository of that information.

Our Commitment

Data can represent people, whakapapa, relationships, knowledge, experiences, and connections with the natural world. Information relating to Māori can carry individual and collective cultural significance and must be treated with respect, care, authority, and context.

This policy applies to available Ōkoro Android features and retained records. Phone/tablet version 13 excludes the development menstrual tracker and watch workout import. Android TV is a separate lunar and cultural display without health or journal features. References to legacy records do not advertise a current tracking feature.

Rangatiratanga — Authority

You remain in control of your journals, tracker and health information, environmental observations, saved places, cultural profiles, Moon Lab material, and other personal content. The Developer does not claim ownership of that information.

You decide what to enter, retain, change, export, synchronise, or delete; which Android and Health Connect permissions to grant; whether coordinates may be disclosed to an environmental service; and which document provider, if any, stores a culture-only sync file.

Whakapapa — Context Matters

Maramataka observations, health and cycle information, environmental records, locations, cultural preferences, and whakapapa-related material do not exist in isolation. Ōkoro keeps personal context local and does not send it to the Developer for profiling, analytics, advertising, artificial-intelligence training, or unrelated commercial use.

Export can remove information from its original device and context. The person exporting it is shown where the file is going and remains responsible for appropriate authority, consent, interpretation, and onward sharing.

Manaakitanga — Respect and Care

Respect begins by not collecting what the Developer does not need. Personal information is processed locally. Journals, sensitive tracker information, and Health Connect data are not sent to Open-Meteo, document sync, widgets, Wear OS, or Android TV unless a user deliberately invokes a feature whose visible purpose requires the selected content.

Coordinates are sent to Open-Meteo when the external-disclosure control is enabled. Separately enabled maps and place search send map-area requests to OpenStreetMap and search text to the device geocoder. Providers may retain IP addresses and request logs. User-directed exports may include personal content if a cloud document provider is selected. See the Android Privacy Policy for data flows, retention and provider policies.

Kaitiakitanga — Responsible Design

  • No Developer account or personal-data database.
  • Local Room and DataStore persistence for personal and cultural settings.
  • Explicit, revocable Android and Health Connect permissions.
  • External coordinate disclosure off by default.
  • Explicit Android document-picker import and export.
  • Culture-only linked documents rather than silent personal-record synchronisation.
  • Local deletion controls and clear provider boundaries.
  • Visible source, freshness, attribution, and safety limitations for environmental information.

Tikanga and Mātauranga Māori

Te reo Māori, maramataka names, traditions, and practices may differ between iwi, hapū, whānau, rohe, and knowledge holders. Ōkoro does not claim that one system is universally authoritative.

Cultural profiles and Moon Lab tools exist so people can preserve appropriate local naming, guidance, sources, and context. The ability to edit or import material does not transfer authority over that knowledge or permit users to misrepresent its source, mandate, or cultural status.

Community Knowledge and Provenance

Communities and knowledge holders retain rights in contributed material unless rights are expressly transferred in writing. Inclusion in Ōkoro does not make mātauranga public domain and does not permit extraction, decontextualisation, or commercial exploitation.

Authored datasets should carry source, contributor, rohe or tradition context, permissions, version, and any restrictions needed for responsible use. Future research or partnerships involving Māori data require appropriate consultation, authority, transparency, consent, and benefit.

Storage, Documents, and Providers

Personal and cultural information is stored locally. A culture-only sync document may be placed in a provider such as Google Drive or OneDrive only after the user selects and links it. That provider then processes the document under its own policies; it does not become a Developer-controlled database.

Personal journals, Health Connect information, menstrual or other tracker records, and private insights are excluded from culture-only sync. Explicit full-data exports can contain user-selected records and must be protected by the user according to their sensitivity and collective obligations.

Environmental and Health Information

Open-Meteo and other external providers receive only what is required for the requested environmental feature. The Developer does not intentionally transmit journals, health information, tracker records, or cultural profiles to them.

Health Connect permissions remain user-controlled and authorised information is processed locally. The Developer never sells health or cultural-profile information because the Developer never receives it.

Commercial Use

Ōkoro does not sell identifiable personal information, behavioural data, health information, location histories, cultural profiles, or community knowledge. A purchase of the App or premium features grants access to software functionality; it does not transfer ownership of Māori data, mātauranga Māori, contributor rights, or user-created content.

Your Control

Where supported, you can view, edit, export, or delete local content; unlink culture documents; withdraw permissions; disable external coordinate disclosure; manage files in the chosen document provider; clear App storage; or uninstall the App. Provider copies and backups must be managed through those services.

Transparency and Change

Ōkoro aims to explain what a feature uses, its purpose, where processing occurs, which provider is involved, and how the feature can be disabled or its information removed. Material changes to these practices will be published before they take effect and any required consent will be requested.
