Legal and Privacy

Māori Data Sovereignty Policy

How Ōkoro zero-collection and on-device design protects the authority, dignity, context, and sovereignty of personal information and Māori data.

Effective 27 July 2026

Using Ōkoro on Android? Read the separate Android Māori Data Sovereignty Policy.

Our commitment: Through the App, the Developer does not collect, receive, store, transmit to the Developer, sell, or share your personal information.

Our Commitment

Ōkoro is built on the understanding that data is more than information. It can represent people, whakapapa, relationships, knowledge, experiences, and connections with the natural world. Information relating to Māori carries personal, collective, and cultural significance and must be treated with respect, care, and responsibility.

Ōkoro supports rangatiratanga through a zero personal-data collection design. Personal and cultural information used by the App remains on your device or, where you choose, within Apple services under your control.

This policy applies to Ōkoro on supported Apple platforms, including iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Watch, and Apple TV.

Our Principles

Rangatiratanga - Authority

You remain in control of your information. The Developer does not claim ownership of your journals, health information, environmental observations, saved locations, cultural preferences, or other personal content.

You decide what information you enter, keep, or delete; which device permissions you grant; whether Apple services such as iCloud are enabled; and whether you share information outside the App.

Whakapapa - Context Matters

Data does not exist in isolation. Health information, maramataka observations, journals, environmental recordings, location information, whakapapa, and cultural preferences carry context.

Ōkoro keeps this information on your device and does not send it to the Developer for profiling, analytics, advertising, commercial exploitation, or any unrelated purpose.

Manaakitanga - Respect and Care

Respect begins by not collecting information the Developer does not need. Personal information is processed locally on your device. Personal journals remain private. Health information remains under your control. If you grant location permission or select a location, Okoro uses it on device and may send approximate or precise coordinates to Apple Weather, Open-Meteo, marine weather, mapping, tide, or astronomy services only to return the requested forecast, attribution, tide, place, or astronomy result. The Developer does not receive, store, sell, or profile this information.

Kaitiakitanga - Responsible Design

Because the Developer does not collect or store personal information, the Developer does not act as a remote repository for your personal or cultural data.

  • Collecting no personal information.
  • Processing personal and cultural information on your device.
  • Requesting only permissions needed for features you choose.
  • Not transmitting journals, health information, or cultural profiles to the Developer.
  • Providing local controls to manage or remove information.
  • Explaining when Apple or an external information service is involved.

Kotahitanga - Benefit

Information used within Ōkoro exists to benefit the person using the App. It supports reconnection with environmental cycles, personal wellbeing, and understanding of relationships between celestial, seasonal, biological, and cultural rhythms.

Tikanga and Mātauranga Māori

Where Māori cultural knowledge is incorporated into Ōkoro, it is treated respectfully. Traditional knowledge, language, names, maramataka, and cultural practices may differ between iwi, hapū, whānau, and knowledge holders. Ōkoro does not present one tradition as universally authoritative.

The App may allow people to maintain their own local naming systems and cultural profiles. This information remains on their device or within an Apple service they choose and is not received by the Developer.

Community Knowledge

Some cultural datasets may be provided for inclusion in the App by communities or individual knowledge holders. Contributors retain their rights and appropriate recognition unless rights are expressly transferred in writing.

The Developer does not claim ownership over contributed mātauranga merely because it is available through Ōkoro. Access to the App does not grant permission to remove cultural knowledge from its context, misrepresent its authority or source, or exploit it commercially.

Any future research or community partnership involving Māori data will require appropriate consultation, transparency, authority, and consent.

Location and Environmental Information

If you grant location permission or select a location, Okoro uses it on device and may send approximate or precise coordinates to Apple Weather, Open-Meteo, marine weather, mapping, tide, or astronomy services only to return the requested forecast, attribution, tide, place, or astronomy result. The Developer does not receive, store, sell, or profile this information.

Apple Weather, Open-Meteo, marine weather, mapping, tide, astronomy, or other external information providers may process requests under their own terms and privacy policies. The Developer does not intentionally send your journals, health information, cultural profile, or other personal content to those services.

Health Information

If you enable HealthKit or another health feature, you control which permissions are granted. Authorised information is processed locally on your device, is not received or stored by the Developer, is not used for advertising or analytics, and access can be withdrawn through Apple settings.

The Developer never sells health information because the Developer never receives it.

Artificial Intelligence

AI-assisted features are designed to assist rather than replace your knowledge and judgement. Personal journals, health information, and cultural preferences used by an AI-assisted feature are processed on your device.

The Developer does not receive this information and does not use it to train public or private artificial intelligence models.

If a future AI feature would require personal information to leave your device, it will not be enabled under this policy unless the policy is first updated and you are given a clear choice before transmission.

Local Storage and Apple Services

Settings, preferences, journals, saved locations, cultural profiles, and other content may be stored locally on your device. If you enable an Apple synchronisation or backup feature, information may be stored in your personal iCloud account and handled by Apple under Apple terms and privacy policy. The Developer does not operate iCloud, receive that information, or have access to your personal iCloud content.

Your Rights and Control

Because the Developer holds no personal information about you, there is no Developer-held personal record to access, export, correct, or delete.

You control information on your device. Where supported, you can view, change, export, or delete local content; withdraw permissions; disconnect Apple services; manage iCloud data; or delete the App.

Commercial Use

Ōkoro does not sell identifiable personal information, behavioural data, health information, location information, or cultural profiles. It cannot sell information it does not collect.

The App does not use personal information for advertising, marketing profiles, or analytics.

Transparency

Ōkoro aims to explain what information a feature uses, why it is needed, whether it remains on your device, whether an Apple or external information service is involved, and how you can disable the feature or remove local information.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the App, law, or Indigenous Data Sovereignty practice evolves. Any revised policy will be published within the App or on our website with an updated effective date.

If a future feature would involve the Developer collecting personal information, this policy and the Privacy Policy will be updated before collection begins and any required permission or consent will be requested.

Our Continuing Commitment

Ōkoro exists to strengthen the relationship between people and the natural world. Technology should empower rather than exploit.

We are committed to protecting the dignity, integrity, context, and sovereignty of information by leaving personal and cultural data under the control of the person who generates it.
