Maramataka Culture

The Okoro Maramataka Culture is unique to Okoro. It was created by the developer from multiple Te Tai Tokerau/Northland datasets, family libraries, and active practice of Maramataka by the developer.

The app recognises that iwi, hapū, whānau, regions, and knowledge holders may maintain different naming systems, meanings, and practices. Okoro built-in profile is one living cultural profile, not a claim that there is one universal Maramataka.

Moon Lab tools let users compare, duplicate, import, export, and create naming profiles so local knowledge can remain contextual and attributed where appropriate.

Maramataka icons mark observation prompts, activity cues, caution, rest, and any custom icons added through Moon Lab. They no longer label lunar days as low, steady, or high energy by default.

Personal energy is recorded only when the user logs it in Journal or tracker views. Okoro can compare those logs with moon phase, weather, tides, HealthKit, and other context, but the app does not assume a lunar day has a fixed energy level for everyone.

What the Maramataka Icons Mean

Icons are prompts for observation and activity context. They are not energy ratings, and they should be read alongside the day notes, your local knowledge, and your own journal entries.

Observe

Look closely and record what you notice.

Caution

Page Guide

Home

Home brings together today's lunar day, moon phase, MoonHero gestures, quick actions, weather, tides, and the stack cards selected for your lifestyle profile.

MoonHero

Drag across the moon area to scrub time. Double-tap the moon to move between the focused moon view, Lunar HUD, and premium Moon Finder when unlocked.

Stack

Lifestyle profiles choose the first stack cards for your day. In Settings you can reorder stack cards, hide cards you do not use, and choose quick actions.

Tides

Tides shows the selected or nearest tide station, daily tide graph, tide tables, weekly strip, datum, and confidence notes from the configured source.

Weather

Weather shows current conditions, hourly and weekly forecast context, and the active weather provider used by environmental recommendations.

Journal

Journal captures observations, photos, mood, notes, and reflections. These entries stay local unless you choose to export or sync data.

Insights

Insights shows the personal rhythm score, the reasons behind it, recommendations, trends, learning controls, HealthKit summary, and safety notes.

Premium Views

Premium unlocks the full Lunar HUD, Moon Finder, Moon Lab tools, deeper health rhythm views, and expanded widget, watch, and complication information.

Settings

Settings manages lifestyle profile, background, menu order, Lunar HUD controls, stack cards, Moon Lab profiles, watch sync, tides, exports, HealthKit, weather, sync, and Help & Support.

HealthKit

HealthKit is optional and permission-based. When enabled, Okoro can read supported Apple Health data such as activity, heart and body signals, sleep, cycle and symptom context, mindful minutes, and State of Mind where available.

HealthKit data improves Okoro by giving the rhythm views real body context. Sleep quality, resting heart rate, HRV, movement, workouts, mindful minutes, cycle day, and mood context can be shown beside journal observations and environmental conditions.

For the best results, wear Apple Watch or keep Apple Health updated, grant only the categories you are comfortable sharing, log mood and cycle notes consistently, refresh Health after permissions change, and review the Why section on the Health page to see what influenced the score.

Okoro can write mindful sessions and State of Mind entries only after permission is granted. You can revoke Health permissions in Apple Health or device Settings at any time.

Premium

Okoro offers a lifetime unlock and a monthly auto-renewable subscription. Either option unlocks the same Premium Okoro feature set.

Premium unlocks the full Lunar HUD overlays, Moon Finder from MoonHero, Moon Lab tools, premium health integrations where available, and expanded watch, widget, and complication information across your devices.

Use Manage Subscription to view, change, or cancel the auto-renewable subscription through the App Store. Use Restore Purchases if a valid purchase or subscription is not reflected inside Okoro.

The premium entitlement is shared with widgets and watch complications through the app group. After unlocking, open Okoro once on the device, then allow WidgetKit a short time to refresh timelines.

If a premium view, widget, or complication still appears locked, open Settings, use Restore in the Premium card, confirm the purchase is active on the App Store account, and reopen the app.

Connected Apps and Permissions

Apple Health

HealthKit is permission-based. Review or revoke Okoro health permissions in Apple Health or device Settings.

Apple Account

The App Store handles purchases, subscriptions, renewals, refunds, and payment methods for Okoro.

iCloud

Local app data may be included in Apple backups or sync only when you enable those Apple services.

Location Services

Location permission is optional and only supports local astronomy, tide, weather, map, and environmental calculations.

Photos

Photo access is used only when you choose to attach or export images from journal and observation workflows.

Calendar

Calendar access is used only when you choose to add Okoro events through the app calendar action.

Widgets and CarPlay

To add an iPhone Home Screen widget, touch and hold the Home Screen, tap Add, search for Okoro, choose a widget size, then place it where you want it.

To add a Lock Screen widget, touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customise, choose the widget area, then add Okoro if it is available for that slot.

Today View and StandBy use the same iPhone widget system. Add Okoro from the widget gallery wherever iOS offers the Okoro widget family you want.

In CarPlay, configure the Okoro iPhone widget where CarPlay offers widgets. Okoro does not need to be opened as a separate CarPlay app for widget content to appear.

If widget content looks stale after changing location, tides, language, background, or premium status, open Okoro on iPhone once so the shared data and widget timelines can refresh.

Apple Watch

Okoro for Apple Watch is installed through the normal companion app flow. It may install automatically when the iPhone app is installed if automatic app install is enabled in the Watch app, but premium unlock itself does not install the watch app.

If Okoro is not on Apple Watch, open the Watch app on iPhone, find Okoro in Available Apps or Installed on Apple Watch, then install or confirm it is enabled.

Premium unlock syncs the entitlement and shared Moon context used by Apple Watch complications. Open Okoro on iPhone after unlocking so the watch app and complications can receive the latest shared state.

To add the complication, edit the watch face on Apple Watch or in the Watch app on iPhone, choose a supported complication slot, then select Okoro.

If a complication still shows locked, restore purchases in Okoro on iPhone, open the iPhone app once, then give WidgetKit a short time to refresh the watch complication.
