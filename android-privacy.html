Privacy Policy

Ōkoro is designed so your personal and cultural information remains under your control and is not collected by the Developer.

Effective 13 August 2026

Our Privacy Promise

Through the App, Ōkoro does not collect, receive, store, transmit to the Developer, sell, or share your personal information.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to Ōkoro on supported Android phones and tablets, Wear OS devices, Android TV and Google TV devices, Android widgets, tiles, complications, and related Android experiences (the “App”). Platform features differ, but the same Developer zero-collection commitment applies.

2. Developer Collection and Accounts

The Developer is James Eruera. The App does not require an Ōkoro account and the Developer does not operate a user database.

The Developer does not collect or receive your name, email address, contacts, passwords, location, health or cycle information, journal content, photographs, observations, saved places, cultural profiles, device or advertising identifiers, usage analytics, diagnostics, searches, preferences, payment-card information, or financial information through the App.

3. On-Device Information

Settings, preferences, journals, tracker records, photographs selected for entries, saved locations, cultural profiles, Moon Lab content, and related information are stored and processed locally using Android application storage. Widgets, Wear OS, and Android TV may receive the limited local context needed to display their enabled features.

The Developer does not receive this information. Android device security, backup, and transfer features may handle application information under settings and terms controlled by you and the relevant platform provider.

4. Location and External Environmental Services

You may use presets or manual coordinates without granting device-location permission. If you request approximate device location, Android asks for permission and uses the result for local astronomy, maramataka, weather, tide, and place calculations.

Ōkoro has a separate external-location disclosure control that is off by default. Only when you turn it on may the selected latitude and longitude be sent over HTTPS to Open-Meteo and Open-Meteo Marine to return weather or marine information. Results are cached locally. The Developer does not receive, store, sell, or profile the coordinates or results.

Bundled LINZ tide predictions can operate locally without sending coordinates to Open-Meteo. External providers process requests under their own terms and privacy policies. Ōkoro does not intentionally send journals, health information, tracker records, or cultural profiles to those services.

5. Health Connect and Sensitive Records

If you connect Health Connect, Android shows the health-data permissions requested by the App. Authorised information is read or written only for features you choose and is processed locally. You can withdraw permissions in Health Connect or Android settings.

Health, menstrual-cycle, pregnancy, menopause, journal, and insight information is not transmitted to or stored by the Developer, used for advertising, or sold. Optional private-view locking uses your Android device credential; the Developer does not receive that credential.

6. Import, Export, and Document Sync

Import and export occur only after an action you initiate through Android’s document picker. Exported files may contain information you selected, so you control where they are saved and with whom they are shared.

Moon Lab may link a culture-only synchronisation document in Google Drive, OneDrive, or another Android document provider selected by you. The provider handles that document under its own terms. Personal journals, health information, and tracker records are not silently uploaded by Ōkoro.

7. Notifications, Widgets, Wear OS, and TV

If enabled, reminders and widget, tile, complication, watch, or TV surfaces may display maramataka, astronomy, weather, tide, or user-configured information. Content shown on shared screens may be visible to other people with access to those devices. You control these surfaces and notification permissions through the App and Android settings.

8. Purchases

Google Play processes Android purchases and subscriptions. The Developer does not receive or store your full payment-card details. Google may provide purchase status needed to unlock features under Google’s terms and privacy policy.

9. Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

The App does not include Developer-operated or third-party analytics, advertising networks, marketing identifiers, or cross-app or cross-website tracking. Google and device manufacturers may independently process Play Store, security, backup, or diagnostic information under controls and policies they provide.

10. Retention, Security, and Deletion

Local information remains until you delete it using available App controls, clear the App’s storage, remove linked documents from their selected provider, or uninstall the App, subject to any device backups you control. Exported files remain wherever you placed them.

Because the Developer holds no personal database, there is no Developer-held personal record to access, correct, export, or delete. Protect your devices, accounts, screen lock, backups, and exported files.

11. Children

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children because the Developer does not collect personal information through the App. Device owners and guardians remain responsible for permissions, shared devices, and external services.

12. Māori Data Sovereignty

Ōkoro’s treatment of Māori data and mātauranga Māori is explained in the Android Māori Data Sovereignty Policy. Local-first processing, explicit export, and user-controlled external disclosure support rangatiratanga.

13. Support Communications

If you email the Developer, the Developer receives the address and information you voluntarily provide outside the App and uses it to respond and maintain necessary support records. Do not include passwords, payment details, health records, journals, or other unnecessary sensitive information.

14. Changes

This policy may be updated when features, law, or privacy practices change. The revised policy will be published with a new effective date. If a future feature would involve Developer collection of personal information, this policy will be updated before collection and any required choice or consent will be requested.
