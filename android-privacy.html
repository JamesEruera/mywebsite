Privacy Policy

Your personal records stay under your control. Learn what stays on your device and what optional services receive.

Updated 23 November 2026

Our Privacy Promise

Your journals and Health Connect summaries are processed on your device, not in a Developer-operated personal-data database. Optional online services and exports can send selected information to providers you choose or enable, as explained below. We do not sell personal or health information or use health records for advertising.

1. Scope and release

This policy describes Ōkoro for Android phones and tablets, including phone release 0.1.11 (version code 12), its widgets and related local features. This release offers optional read-only Health Connect summaries. It does not request menstruation access, offer the development menstrual tracker, or import watch workouts into Health Connect. Older installed or test versions may differ; removing a feature does not automatically delete records that you previously stored or exported.

2. Developer and accounts

The Developer is James Eruera. Ōkoro does not require an Ōkoro account and does not upload your personal records to a Developer-operated database. Google Play accounts and purchases, optional online providers, documents you choose to export, and communications you send to support have separate processing described below.

3. Information on your device

Journals, mood and energy observations entered in journals, selected-photo references, preferences, selected locations and cultural profiles are stored in Android application storage. Astronomy and Maramataka calculations use your selected location. Existing records imported from older versions, including development tracker records, may remain in local storage and full backups; the phone release does not expose a tracker or include those tracker records in production Insights. The app disables Android automatic app backup and device-transfer backup through its manifest and backup rules. Explicit exports remain available.

4. Optional location, weather, maps and search

You can use a preset or enter coordinates without device-location permission. Android approximate-location permission is requested only when you choose to use device location. A manually selected pin or entered coordinates may be more precise than approximate device location.

The external weather/tide sharing control is off by default. If enabled, selected latitude and longitude are sent over HTTPS to Open-Meteo and Open-Meteo Marine to retrieve environmental information. Requests also expose the network IP address and normal request metadata to the provider. Bundled LINZ tide predictions can run locally.

Map and place search have a separate enable action and disclosure. When enabled, OpenStreetMap receives requested map tiles identifying the viewed area, your IP address and request metadata. Place-search text is sent to the device's geocoding provider. Selecting a distant place does not necessarily reveal your physical location, but selecting your home or current position can do so.

These providers process requests under their own policies. Open-Meteo's paid API policy describes IP-address and request-URL logs retained for 90 days before individual logs are removed; URLs can include requested coordinates. OpenStreetMap also records network-access information. We therefore do not describe all online requests as memory-only or anonymous. See Open-Meteo privacy information and OpenStreetMap's privacy policy. The app does not send journal or Health Connect content to these environmental services.

5. Health Connect: read-only and optional

With active Premium access and your permission, Ōkoro reads supported Health Connect records from the previous seven days to display factual summaries on Stack and Insights. It reads steps, sleep duration, exercise-session duration, distance and active calories for totals, and heart-rate samples, resting-heart-rate records and heart-rate variability (RMSSD) for recorded averages. These summaries help you review recent activity, sleep and heart information alongside your own observations.

Each record category requires its individual permission. The app does not measure these values itself. Without Premium, new summary reads stop even if Android permissions remain granted. You can manage or revoke permissions through Health Connect without Premium. Core lunar, Maramataka and Journal features do not require Health Connect.

This phone release does not request health-write permissions or menstruation permissions. It does not provide watch workout import, continuous heart monitoring, emergency alerts, diagnosis or treatment. Health Connect records used for these summaries are processed on the phone, not uploaded to the Developer or sent to advertising services. Revoking permission stops future access; it does not delete the source records in Health Connect. Manage those records in Health Connect.

Ōkoro is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent medical conditions. Consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice. Maramataka guidance and journal associations are not evidence that lunar phases cause health changes.

6. Exports, imports and linked documents

An export or backup can contain personal journal content, selected locations, preferences and any legacy records retained in the database. Exported records may include health-related observations or previously imported data. Export files are not encrypted by Ōkoro. Save them only to destinations you trust.

Android's document picker lets you choose local storage or a document provider, which may include your own cloud account. If you choose a cloud destination, its provider may receive and retain the exported content under its own policies. Selecting Export is your instruction to write that file; this is separate from ordinary on-device Health Connect summaries.

Moon Lab can link a culture-only document through a provider you select, such as Google Drive or OneDrive. Synchronisation updates that chosen document. Personal journal and tracker tables are excluded from culture-only sync. Imported files are read to restore or load the content you choose. Unlink documents and manage cloud copies through the relevant app and provider controls.

7. Notifications, widgets and paired devices

Enabled reminders and home-screen widgets may show lunar information on screens visible to other people. Control notification permissions and visible widgets in the app and Android settings.

Compatibility code can send a limited snapshot to your paired Ōkoro watch through Google Play services: selected location and place name, lunar and rise/set information, cultural guidance, language/display preferences and Premium status. That snapshot contains no journal entries or Health Connect summary. This does not enable watch workout import or promise availability of a new Wear OS release. Disconnect paired devices if you do not want companion delivery.

8. Purchases and Premium

Google Play processes the one-time lifetime purchase and monthly subscription. Both unlock the same Premium entitlement in this phone release. The app queries purchase status and acknowledges purchases through Google Play to unlock features. Full payment-card details are handled by Google, not Ōkoro. Manage purchases and subscriptions in Google Play. Ending Premium does not delete your locally stored journal or culture records.

9. Advertising and analytics

The app does not integrate advertising networks or a Developer-operated analytics service. Health and personal records are not sold or used for advertising, profiling or AI training. Online providers may keep operational logs described in their own policies. Google Play and device providers may separately process store, billing, security and diagnostic information.

10. Retention, security and deletion

Local personal records remain until removed with the relevant app controls, by clearing app storage, or by uninstalling. Health summary values are derived from permitted records; their source remains managed by Health Connect. Exports, imported copies and linked cloud documents are separate copies: deleting the app does not delete those files or records held by another provider.

Android app isolation and your device screen lock protect local app access. Optional private-view locking uses the Android device credential; Ōkoro does not receive that credential. Environmental requests use HTTPS. Cloud-document transport and retention depend on the provider you select. Do not treat an exported JSON file as encrypted or protected simply because it came from the app.

For provider-held request logs, use the provider's privacy contact and rights process; we cannot promise immediate deletion of another provider's records. Contact us for help identifying the relevant provider. We have no Developer-operated database of your journal or Health Connect records to erase.

11. Intended audience

The app is intended for people aged 16 and over. Device owners and guardians should consider permissions, shared screens and external providers before enabling optional features.

12. Māori data sovereignty

You retain control of your personal content and cultural material. See our Android Māori Data Sovereignty Policy for the treatment of mātauranga, context, authority and user-directed export.

13. Support and policy updates

If you contact support, we receive the address and content you provide and use it to respond and retain necessary support correspondence. Do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive records. This policy will be updated when processing or features change, with an updated date and any required disclosures or consent.
