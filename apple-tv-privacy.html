Legal and Privacy

Privacy Policy for Ōkoro on Apple TV

Ōkoro for Apple TV does not collect, store, transmit, or share personal information.

Effective 29 June 2026

No Personal Data Collection

Ōkoro for Apple TV ("the App") does not collect personal information, including names or contact details, email addresses or account information, precise location information, device identifiers, usage or behavioural data, advertising information, analytics or diagnostic data, payment information, or financial information.

No Accounts

The App does not require you to create an account or provide personal information.

Location Information

If the App allows you to select or enter a location, that information is processed locally on your Apple TV to provide relevant astronomical or environmental information. We do not receive, collect, or store your selected location.

Local Data

App settings and preferences may be stored locally on your Apple TV. This information remains on your device and is not transmitted to us. You can remove locally stored information by deleting the App or clearing its data through your Apple TV settings, where available.

Analytics and Tracking

The App does not use third-party analytics services, advertising networks, tracking technologies, marketing identifiers, or cross-app or cross-website tracking.

In-App Purchases

If the App offers purchases or subscriptions, they are processed securely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment card details. Apple handles purchase information according to Apple privacy policy.

Third-Party Services

The App may request non-personal astronomical, weather, tide, or environmental information from external services. We do not intentionally send personal information to these services. Requests are limited to the information necessary to provide the requested feature. Apple platform services used by the App are governed by Apple privacy policy.

Children Privacy

Because the App does not collect personal information, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Security

Since we do not collect or store personal information, we do not maintain a database of user information. Information stored locally on your Apple TV is protected by the security features provided by Apple and tvOS.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the App features or privacy practices change. Any updated policy will be published within the App or on our website with a revised effective date.
