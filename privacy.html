Privacy Policy

Okoro is designed so your personal information remains under your control and is not collected by the Developer.

Effective 27 July 2026

Our Privacy Promise

Through the App, Okoro does not collect, receive, store, transmit to the Developer, sell, or share your personal information.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to Okoro on supported Apple platforms, including iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Watch, and Apple TV ("the App"). Platform features may differ, but the same zero personal-data collection commitment applies.

2. No Personal Data Collection

The Developer does not collect or receive names, email addresses, contact details, user accounts, passwords, precise or approximate location information, health, fitness, wellbeing, journal, biological information, device identifiers, advertising identifiers, usage, behavioural, analytics, diagnostic data, searches, preferences, saved places, cultural settings, payment-card information, or financial information.

The App does not maintain a Developer-controlled database of users or personal information.

3. No Accounts

The App does not require you to create an Okoro account or provide personal information to the Developer.

4. On-Device Processing

Features that use your selections, preferences, journal entries, cultural settings, health information, or location process that information locally on your device. The Developer does not receive it.

Where the operating system gives you a permission choice, you control whether the App may access the relevant information. You can change permissions through your device settings.

5. Location Information

If you allow location access or select a location, it is used on your device to calculate or display relevant astronomical, maramataka, weather, tide, or environmental information. The Developer does not receive, collect, or store your location.

6. Health Information

If you enable HealthKit or another health feature, authorised information is read and processed locally on your device only for the feature you select. It is not transmitted to or stored by the Developer, used for advertising, sold, or shared by the Developer.

You control health permissions through Apple settings and may withdraw access at any time.

7. Local Storage and iCloud

App settings, preferences, journals, saved locations, cultural profiles, and other content may be stored locally on your device. Where you choose to enable an Apple synchronisation or backup feature, information may be stored in your personal iCloud account and handled by Apple under Apple terms and privacy policy.

The Developer does not operate the iCloud service, receive that information, or have access to your personal iCloud content. You can remove local information by using available deletion controls or deleting the App. iCloud information can be managed through your Apple account and device settings.

8. Analytics, Diagnostics, Advertising, and Tracking

The App does not include Developer-operated or third-party analytics, advertising networks, marketing identifiers, tracking technologies, or cross-app or cross-website tracking.

Apple may independently make device diagnostics or App Store information available under settings and terms controlled by Apple. The Developer does not use such information to identify or profile you.

9. External Information Services

The App may request astronomical, weather, tide, mapping, or environmental information from Apple platform services or other external information providers. Requests are limited to what is necessary to return the feature you requested and are designed not to identify you to the Developer.

Apple and any independent external provider process information under their own terms and privacy policies. The Developer does not intentionally send your journals, health information, cultural profile, or other personal content to those services.

10. Artificial Intelligence

Where the App provides AI-assisted features, personal journals, health information, and cultural preferences are processed on your device. The Developer does not receive that information and does not use it to train public or private artificial intelligence models.

If a future feature would require information to leave your device, it will not be enabled under this policy unless this policy is updated and you are given a clear choice before transmission.

11. Purchases and Subscriptions

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. The Developer does not receive or store your payment-card details. Apple handles transaction information under Apple terms and privacy policy.

12. Children Privacy

Because the Developer does not collect personal information through the App, the App does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

13. Data Security and Deletion

Because the Developer does not collect or store personal information, there is no Developer-held personal database to secure, access, export, correct, or delete. Information on your device is protected by the security features of your Apple device and operating system.

You remain responsible for the security of your device, Apple account, backups, and passcode. You can remove information using the App controls, your device or iCloud settings, or by deleting the App, as applicable.

14. Māori Data Sovereignty

Okoro treatment of Māori data and mātauranga Māori is further explained in the Māori Data Sovereignty Policy. The App zero-collection and on-device design supports rangatiratanga by keeping personal and cultural information under the user control.

15. Support Communications

If you choose to email the Developer for support, the Developer will receive the email address and information you voluntarily include. This occurs outside the App and is used only to respond to your request, maintain necessary support records, and comply with law. Do not include passwords, payment-card details, health information, journals, or other unnecessary sensitive information.

16. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy if the App features, legal requirements, or privacy practices change. An updated policy will be published within the App or on our website with a revised effective date.

If a future feature would involve Developer collection of personal information, this policy will be updated before that collection begins and any required permission or consent will be requested.

17. Apple TV Policy

Okoro for Apple TV has a dedicated privacy policy for the tvOS app experience. Read it at Apple TV Privacy Policy.
